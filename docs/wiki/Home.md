# Day 72: Data Exploration with Pandas

Do college degrees pay you back? This project explores the PayScale post-university salary data by undergraduate major with pandas, in a Jupyter notebook backed by a small tested Python package.

## The five questions

1. Which degrees have the highest starting salaries? Physician Assistant, at 74,300.
2. Which majors have the lowest earnings? Spanish starts lowest (34,000) and Education is lowest in mid-career (52,000).
3. Which degrees have the highest earning potential? Economics, Finance and Chemical Engineering lead the 90th percentile.
4. Which majors are lowest risk? Nursing has the smallest spread between low and high earners.
5. Do Business, STEM or HASS degrees earn more? STEM, on average, then Business, then HASS.
